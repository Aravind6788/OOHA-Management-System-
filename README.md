PROBLEM STATEMENT

Out-of-Home (OOH) advertising remains one of the most impactful and widely used marketing methods for brands aiming to reach mass audiences. With hoardings, billboards, and banners located in strategic public spaces, OOH campaigns have the power to create strong brand visibility. However, despite its effectiveness, the management and execution of OOH advertising are still rooted in outdated, manual practices. Advertising agencies and clients continue to depend on physical visits, telephone calls, and email communications to manage bookings, inquire about availability, and finalize campaigns — a process that is inefficient, time-consuming, and prone to miscommunication.

The traditional workflow lacks a centralized platform for managing advertising inventory, making it difficult for agencies to maintain and present an up-to-date catalog of their hoarding boards. Similarly, clients have no streamlined way to browse available boards, view detailed information such as location, pricing, or dimensions, or submit inquiries efficiently. As a result, a significant amount of time and resources are wasted on repetitive coordination, manual data sharing, and follow-ups. This often leads to missed opportunities, delayed campaign launches, and an overall unsatisfactory user experience for both agencies and advertisers.

Moreover, the absence of a transparent and data-driven system prevents both parties from making informed decisions. Clients cannot easily visualize the location of boards or evaluate them comparatively, while agencies have limited tools to showcase their full inventory or respond promptly to potential leads. The lack of automation also hinders scalability — as the number of boards or clients increases, so does the complexity of managing everything manually.

In a digital-first world, where businesses are increasingly adopting cloud-based and automated platforms, the OOH advertising industry is in urgent need of technological transformation. There is a clear demand for a solution that simplifies the end-to-end process, brings transparency to inventory management, improves communication between stakeholders, and supports growth through digital innovation.

The proposed solution, OOHA Management System, aims to address these challenges by offering a centralized, web-based SaaS platform where agencies can list and manage their hoarding boards, while clients can easily discover, inquire, and book boards online. By eliminating the friction in traditional OOH advertising workflows, OOHA Management System not only enhances operational efficiency but also creates new opportunities for agencies to scale their business in a smarter and more sustainable way. 

EMPATHY

In any successful product development process, understanding the users' pain points, challenges, and needs is critical. Empathy plays a vital role in designing a solution that resonates with real-world problems and offers meaningful value to its users. In the context of OOHA Management System, the users include advertising agencies, media planners, marketing executives, and brand promoters who are actively involved in Out-of-Home (OOH) advertising. Each of these users faces distinct yet overlapping frustrations with the current system, which remains largely dependent on manual operations and fragmented communication.

For advertising agencies, managing multiple hoarding boards without a proper digital inventory system is a daily struggle. They often have to maintain large amounts of physical data in spreadsheets or offline documents, which increases the risk of errors, delays, and mismanagement. Moreover, showcasing available boards to clients is cumbersome and time-intensive, as it usually involves phone calls, emails, or in-person meetings. These agencies often miss out on potential clients simply because their offerings are not visible or easily accessible online.

From the client's perspective especially brand managers or businesses looking to advertise the lack of a unified platform to explore hoardings and book advertising spaces adds to their frustration. They must rely heavily on agency representatives to provide information, and they often experience delays in getting responses or quotations. The inability to view hoarding locations visually, compare prices, or check availability in real time makes the entire decision-making process longer and less efficient. Additionally, clients want transparency and convenience, especially when they are planning campaigns on tight deadlines or limited budgets.

Furthermore, both clients and agencies desire better communication and faster turnaround times. In today’s fast-paced business environment, there is little tolerance for back-and-forth emails, manual paperwork, or miscommunication regarding bookings. Users expect digital solutions that are intuitive, responsive, and streamlined to save time, reduce confusion, and enhance trust.

By putting ourselves in the shoes of these users, we understand that their needs are not just about a digital interface — they seek clarity, speed, control, and accessibility in managing OOH advertising campaigns. OOHA Management System was built with these needs at the core, aiming to eliminate existing pain points, simplify communication, and offer a seamless experience that aligns with modern digital expectations. Through empathy-driven design, OOHA Management System provides real value to its users by understanding their daily challenges and delivering a solution tailored to their real-world scenarios.

PROBLEM DEFINITION

The Out-of-Home (OOH) advertising industry continues to rely on conventional, manual processes for managing and showcasing hoarding boards. Advertising agencies often maintain their inventory in offline records or spreadsheets, leading to disorganized data, inefficient communication, and limited visibility of available ad spaces. Clients and brand promoters face difficulties in accessing accurate, up-to-date information about hoardings, including location, availability, pricing, and visual representation. The lack of a centralized digital system results in miscommunication, time delays, and missed business opportunities.

The absence of an integrated platform to connect agencies with advertisers creates a fragmented ecosystem, where campaign planning becomes cumbersome and time-consuming. Manual inquiries, unstructured coordination, and poor transparency reduce operational efficiency and limit the scalability of agencies. There is also no interactive or visual means for clients to evaluate hoarding options, making it harder to make informed decisions quickly.

Therefore, there is a strong need for a digital platform that centralizes hoarding information, streamlines client-agency communication, enables visual mapping of advertising locations, and automates the booking and inquiry process. The solution should aim to bridge the gap between traditional OOH advertising workflows and modern digital practices, ultimately creating a more transparent, efficient, and scalable system for all stakeholders.


USER REQUIREMENTS

User requirements define what functionalities and features the users expect from the system to meet their needs. For OOHA Management System, the users mainly include advertising agencies, brand promoters, marketing professionals, and media planners. These users seek a platform that is easy to use, efficient, and capable of simplifying the entire OOH advertising process. Below is a breakdown of the key user requirements for OOHA Management System
1. User Registration and Authentication	
Users should be able to register on the platform by providing basic details such as their name, email, phone number, and company information. The platform should offer secure login and authentication mechanisms for both agencies and clients to ensure data protection. Additionally, users should have the ability to reset their passwords and manage their account details securely.
2. Digital Hoarding Board Listing
Agencies should have the ability to list their available hoardings by uploading comprehensive details such as location, size, image, pricing, duration, and availability. Each hoarding board should be assigned a unique ID to ensure it is easy to manage, update, or remove as needed.
3. Search and Filter Functionality
Clients should be able to search and filter hoarding boards using various parameters such as city, location, price, type (static or digital), and availability. To enhance user experience, a map-based interface should be provided to help users visualize the exact location of the hoardings.
4. Interactive Dashboard
Both clients and agencies should have access to personalized dashboards tailored to their roles. Clients should be able to view their inquiries, booked boards, and communication history, while agencies should have the ability to track all their listed boards, monitor incoming inquiries, and check the status of bookings.
5. Inquiry and Booking Management
Clients should be able to raise inquiries on specific hoarding boards directly through the platform. Agencies must receive real-time notifications of these inquiries and be able to respond within the system. Additionally, the platform should support basic booking workflows, including status updates such as pending, approved, and completed.
6. Secure Communication System
The platform should include a built-in messaging or communication module to facilitate seamless interaction between clients and agencies. It should also provide both email and in-platform alerts for important actions such as inquiry updates, confirmations, and new listings.
7. Data Privacy and Security
Users expect their data, including business details, bookings, and communication, to be securely stored and managed on the platform. To ensure data privacy and protection, role-based access control should be implemented to restrict access to sensitive information based on user roles.
8. Scalability and Usability
The platform interface should be clean, intuitive, and easy to navigate, accommodating users with varying levels of technical expertise. As the number of users or listings increases, the system must maintain responsiveness and efficiency to ensure a smooth user experience.

1.6 OBJECTIVE OF THIS PROJECT
The primary objective of the OOHA Management System project is to transform the traditional Out-of-Home (OOH) advertising landscape by developing a comprehensive, user-friendly, and digital platform that streamlines the management, booking, and communication processes for advertising agencies and clients.
The OOHA Management System is designed to modernize and digitize the traditional Out-of-Home (OOH) advertising process. By creating a centralized digital platform, it enables advertising agencies to manage and showcase their hoarding board inventory efficiently. Clients, in turn, can easily browse, filter, compare, and select advertising spaces online without relying on offline interactions. This digitization promotes a streamlined experience, saving time and improving accessibility. It also reduces dependence on manual communication methods and paperwork. The platform aims to make the entire hoarding selection process smoother for clients and more manageable for agencies. It supports uploading detailed board information, including size, price, availability, and visuals. By bringing both parties onto a single platform, the system enhances discoverability and business reach. Ultimately, it bridges the gap between demand and supply in the OOH advertising space with ease. This ensures increased engagement and operational efficiency for all users involved.
A major objective of the platform is to enhance communication and ensure transparency between clients and agencies. Traditional methods often involve lengthy email threads or phone calls that can lead to misunderstandings and delays. The OOHA Management System replaces this with real-time updates and a secure, built-in messaging module. Clients can raise inquiries on specific boards, while agencies receive instant notifications and respond within the platform. This fosters clear, trackable communication, reducing the chance of missed updates or miscommunication. Email and in-platform alerts keep users informed of key actions like new listings, booking confirmations, or inquiry responses. The transparency also builds trust between clients and agencies, improving business relationships. Additionally, the system supports booking status updates such as pending, approved, or completed, keeping all parties aligned. By centralizing all conversations and actions, the platform ensures a seamless and accountable workflow. This elevates the professional standard of OOH advertising transactions.
The system also focuses on automating and streamlining key workflows to minimize manual tasks for users. Features like inquiry management, booking automation, and response tracking help reduce the administrative burden on agencies and clients alike. Each board listing is assigned a unique ID, making it easier to manage, update, or remove. The entire process—from listing to final booking—is designed to be handled through the system. Clients can view availability in real-time and make informed decisions quickly. Agencies can manage incoming requests, view booking statuses, and handle multiple boards with minimal effort. This automation not only boosts operational speed but also enhances accuracy by reducing human error. Furthermore, both clients and agencies have personalized dashboards where they can track activities, inquiries, and communications. These features ensure a more organized and efficient OOH advertising ecosystem. The overall goal is to let users focus on value-driven decisions rather than repetitive tasks.
